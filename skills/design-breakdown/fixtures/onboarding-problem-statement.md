# Make onboarding better

**Author:** product@
**Status:** Draft
**Date:** 2026-03-02

## The problem

New users are dropping off during onboarding. We've seen activation rates fall from 62% to 48% over the last two quarters. Sales says enterprise prospects bounce when they hit the workspace-setup step. Support tickets in the "getting started" tag are up 40% YoY.

## Why it matters

Onboarding is the single largest leak in our funnel right now. Fixing it is the highest-leverage thing we can do this half. Every percentage point of activation is worth roughly $1.2M ARR based on current cohort LTV.

## Goals

- Activation rate back above 60% within two quarters.
- Reduce time-to-first-value (TTFV) from current ~14 minutes to under 5.
- Halve the volume of "getting started" support tickets.

## Constraints

- Engineering capacity: ~2 engineers for one quarter.
- Must work for both self-serve and sales-assisted signups.
- Cannot break existing SSO integrations.

## Success criteria

- Activation rate ≥ 60% sustained for 4 weeks.
- TTFV p50 < 5 minutes.
- Support ticket volume in "getting started" tag down 50% MoM.

## Next steps

Design proposal to follow. We need to decide whether to invest in:

- A new guided setup wizard
- Better defaults that skip optional steps
- A redesigned dashboard that meets users where they are
- Some combination of the above
